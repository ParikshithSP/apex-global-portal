Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, disclose, and protect personal information.

Draft notice: This draft is intended as website content and should be reviewed and adapted to reflect the company’s actual practices, locations, and applicable legal requirements before publication.

This Privacy Policy explains how Apex Global (“Apex Global,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) may collect, use, disclose, and protect personal information when you visit our website, contact us, submit a resume, apply for a role, or use our recruitment and staffing services.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect information such as:

  • Contact details, including name, email address, telephone number, and business contact information.
  • Professional and recruitment information, including resume/CV details, employment history, education, skills, qualifications, certifications, portfolio or profile links, and career preferences.
  • Hiring and business information, including company name, job title, role requirements, staffing needs, and communications with our team.
  • Information you provide in forms, messages, interviews, or other communications.
  • Website and technical information, which may include IP address, browser and device details, pages viewed, approximate usage data, and cookie or similar technology data, depending on the tools enabled on the website.

Please do not submit sensitive personal information unless it is specifically requested for a legitimate purpose and you have been given appropriate instructions.

2. How We Use Information

We may use personal information for purposes such as:

  • Responding to inquiries and communicating with clients, candidates, and website visitors.
  • Providing recruitment, staffing, executive search, and workforce solutions.
  • Reviewing candidate profiles and considering individuals for relevant employment opportunities.
  • Managing recruitment processes, interviews, candidate submissions, and hiring coordination.
  • Understanding client hiring requirements and delivering agreed services.
  • Operating, maintaining, securing, and improving our website and services.
  • Managing records, preventing misuse, and meeting applicable legal or regulatory obligations.

3. Legal Basis and Consent

Where applicable law requires a legal basis for processing personal information, we will process it on a basis permitted by that law, such as consent, steps taken at your request, performance of a contract, legitimate interests, or compliance with legal obligations. Where consent is relied upon, you may be able to withdraw it, subject to applicable law and any necessary continued processing.

4. Sharing of Information

We may share personal information, where appropriate and permitted, with relevant parties such as:

  • Clients and prospective employers for recruitment consideration, where relevant to a candidate and consistent with applicable requirements.
  • Service providers supporting website hosting, recruitment systems, communications, security, analytics, or business operations.
  • Professional advisers, regulators, public authorities, or other parties where required by law or necessary to protect rights, safety, or legitimate interests.
  • Other parties with your direction or consent, where applicable.

We do not state that personal information is sold. Any data-sharing practices should be confirmed against Apex Global’s actual operations and applicable law before this policy is published.

5. International Data Transfers

Because recruitment and staffing activities may involve clients, candidates, or service providers in different locations, personal information may be processed or transferred across borders where necessary for the purposes described in this policy. Where required, appropriate safeguards will be used in accordance with applicable law.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, including recruitment, client service, recordkeeping, dispute resolution, and legal or regulatory requirements. Retention periods may vary based on the type of information, the relevant relationship, and applicable law. We will take steps to delete or de-identify information when it is no longer required, where appropriate and legally permitted.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. No method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to support essential functionality, remember preferences, understand website usage, or improve performance. The types of cookies used depend on the tools and settings implemented on the website. Where required, we will provide appropriate notice and obtain consent before using non-essential cookies. You may be able to manage cookies through your browser settings or any cookie preference tool provided on the website.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction or deletion of, or a copy of your personal information; object to or restrict certain processing; withdraw consent; or raise a complaint with a relevant authority. To make a request, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity and may be unable to fulfill certain requests where an exception applies under applicable law.

10. Candidate Information

If you submit a resume or professional profile, we may use the information to assess your suitability for roles, communicate about opportunities, and share relevant details with clients for recruitment consideration. Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and that you have the right to share any information about other individuals. You may contact us to update your profile or ask about its retention.

11. Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those third parties. Please review their privacy notices before providing personal information.

12. Children’s Privacy

Our website and recruitment services are not intended for children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the website. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so that we can review and take appropriate action.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, services, or legal requirements. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised “Last Updated” date.
